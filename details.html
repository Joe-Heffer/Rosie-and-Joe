The Day

Order of the Day

We have put together an idea of what our day will look like.

Arrival

12:30 pm

Eyam Hall

Eyam Hall, Main Road,
Eyam, Hope Valley,
Derbyshire, S32 5QW

Ceremony

1:30 pm

Eyam Hall Gardens

We'll be exchanging our vows in the walled gardens of Eyam Hall, surrounded by the Peak District countryside. Please aim to arrive by 12:30 pm so we can begin together at 1:30 pm.

Drinks & Photographs

2:00 pm

The Courtyard

Eyam Hall Courtyard

After the ceremony, join us for drinks in the courtyard while we have photographs taken with family and friends.

The Hall itself is closed to visitors, but the courtyard beside it is open to the public during the day, with a café and shops, so you may spot other visitors while we have drinks and photographs. It’s ours alone from 5 pm, once the courtyard closes for the day.

Wedding Breakfast

4:00 pm

The Upper Barn

We’ll sit down together for an afternoon wedding breakfast in the Upper Barn, followed by speeches. Wine and soft drinks are on us with dinner.

Our wedding breakfast menu is fully vegetarian and plant-based. Please let us know on your RSVP if you have any other dietary requirements.

Evening Reception

7:00 pm

Music & Dancing

Our evening guests join us as the tables are cleared for music and dancing in the barn, which carries on until 11:30 pm.

After dinner the venue’s bar is open for guests to buy their own drinks, and card payments are accepted.

Carriages

Midnight

A fond farewell

Carriages arrive at midnight, by which time everyone will need to have left the Hall.

There are no Ubers in the Peaks and taxis are limited, so please pre-book yours. See our Getting Here page.

Good to know

A handful of notes about the day and the venue's house rules:

  • The courtyard — The Hall itself is closed to visitors, but the courtyard beside it is open to the public during the day, with a café and shops, so you may spot other visitors during drinks and photographs. It’s ours exclusively from 5 pm, once the courtyard closes for the day.
  • Confetti — we will provide some, so please do not bring your own. The venue has strict rules.
  • Smoking — smoking, including e-cigarettes, is permitted in designated outdoor areas only. The gardens and all indoor spaces are non-smoking.
  • Bar — we are providing drinks for our drinks reception, and wine and soft drinks with dinner. After dinner, the venue has a bar where guests are able to buy their own drinks, and card payments are accepted.
  • End of the night — music will finish at 11:30 pm and carriages will arrive at midnight, by which time everyone will need to have departed. There are no Ubers in the Peaks and taxis are limited, so please pre-book your taxis — see our Getting Here page.
